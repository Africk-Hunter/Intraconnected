## V 1.11 | Vault Notes
image: /images/Lock.svg
A new kind of note for passwords and other secrets. Vault notes get a second lock, a passphrase only you know, so not even we can read them. The vault locks itself when you step away.

## V 1.10 | Faster on Desktop, Easier on Phone
image: /images/Plus.svg
Desktop gets a right-click menu on every idea, keyboard shortcuts, search, undo, and a breadcrumb that keeps your place. On your phone, Back now goes up one level, and every idea has a quick-actions menu with undo.

## V 1.07 | Note Ideas
image: /images/Pen.svg
A new kind of idea built for longer writing: give it a title and a full-length body instead of a single line. Notes get their own typewriter-styled card and can't have child ideas.
